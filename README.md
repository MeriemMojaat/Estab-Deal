# 🛍️ Estab'Deal – Sustainable Student Exchange Platform

A web-based platform developed for ESPRIT students to exchange unused clothing and items, encouraging sustainable habits and reducing waste through a point-based barter system.

# 🌍 Project Vision

Estab'Deal is more than just a clothes-swapping platform; it is a step toward sustainability. The platform enables students to reuse and repurpose unused clothing and items instead of discarding them.

It promotes conscious consumption, encourages second-hand exchange, and supports **SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production**.

# 🧩 How It Works

👕 Students upload photos and descriptions of their unused items.

🔎 Other students can browse, search, and filter available items.

🔄 Students can propose an exchange using one of their own items.

💬 Users can communicate and negotiate to finalize an exchange.

🎯 With every successful trade, users earn points.

🛒 Points can be used to acquire an item even when the student does not have another item to exchange.

✅ Both parties confirm the exchange once an agreement is reached.

⭐ Users can review and rate each other after a successful exchange.

# 🧠 Features

## 👨‍🎓 Client-Side Features

🛂 **ESPRIT Student Authentication** – Secure registration and login restricted to ESPRIT students.

👤 **Student Profile Management** – Manage personal information, profile details, exchange history, and accumulated points.

📤 **Product Upload & Management** – Publish unused items with photos, descriptions, categories, condition, size, and other relevant information.

🔎 **Product Search & Filtering** – Search and filter available items based on category, condition, size, type, and other criteria.

🔄 **Exchange Proposals** – Allow students to propose their own items in exchange for another student's product.

💬 **Communication & Messaging** – Enable students to communicate and negotiate before confirming an exchange.

⭐ **Review & Rating System** – Allow users to review and rate each other after a successful exchange.

🎯 **Point-Based Reward System** – Earn points after successful exchanges and use them to obtain items without providing another item in return.

🛍️ **Product Requests & Orders** – Request or claim available items using either a direct exchange or accumulated points.

✅ **Exchange Confirmation** – Allow both parties to confirm an agreed exchange and update its status.

🔔 **Notifications** – Notify students about exchange proposals, messages, requests, confirmations, and exchange status updates.

📋 **Exchange History** – Track completed, pending, cancelled, and rejected exchanges.

❤️ **Wishlist & Favorites** – Save interesting products for later.

🚩 **Report & Moderation** – Allow students to report inappropriate products or problematic users.

## 🏢 Admin / Company-Side Features

🛡️ **User Management** – Manage student accounts, access, and reported users.

📦 **Product Moderation** – Review, approve, reject, or remove products that violate platform rules.

🔎 **Product Quality Control** – Verify product information and condition when necessary.

🔄 **Exchange Management** – Monitor ongoing and completed exchanges and handle disputes.

📊 **Dashboard & Statistics** – Monitor users, products, exchanges, points, and overall platform activity.

🚩 **Reports & Complaints Management** – Review user reports and take appropriate administrative action.

🎯 **Point Management** – Monitor and manage the platform's point-based transactions.

📢 **Notifications & Announcements** – Send important announcements and notifications to students.

# 🛠️ Tech Stack
